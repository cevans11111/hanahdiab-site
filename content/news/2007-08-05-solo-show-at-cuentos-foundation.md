I have an upcoming solo show at Cuentos Foundation opening October 20th, 2007!

The title is: Recreating Home: Kisses & Borders.

I am very excited, because I love the mission statement for the foundation, and almost all of the paintings are new work--never seen before! This show has has been a great catalyst for me to create this body of work.

Cuentos Foundation is a community-based non profit dedicated to creating and facilitating cross-cultural understanding and exchange through the arts.
Check out their website: [link:http://www.cuentosfoundation.org/events.html]cuentosfoundation.org[/link]
